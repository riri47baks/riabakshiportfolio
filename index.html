How I work

Data, AI & creativity

Read the data first, use AI to move faster, then spend the saved time on the creative idea that actually gets remembered.

Data

Every campaign starts in the numbers (GA4, SEMrush, Tableau, CRM pipelines) to find where CAC is leaking and where a lead is actually worth chasing. Strategy gets built on what the funnel says, not on instinct.

AI

AI tools sit in the daily workflow, not on the side: research, content drafting, creative production, reporting. It's what lets a lean team ship at the pace a Web3 or SaaS founder needs.

Creativity

The output has to earn attention on its own. That means building for the format, not bolting a campaign onto it after the fact.

      A note on my move home

      Elephant in the room

      A reader might wonder about it, so I'll say it first. I spent years building in Southeast Asia, and I'm now finding my footing again in India.

      Read the letter

      Dear reader,

      Here is the thing you may be wondering about, so I'll say it first. For years my work lived in Phnom Penh, Bangkok and Singapore: Forex lead generation across Thailand, Laos and Malaysia, Cambodia's first AR filter for Coca-Cola, regional teams I built and led. Since moving back to India I've been finding my footing again, first through independent consulting, then Web3 business development, and now marketing at Bombay Banta.

      I won't pretend that was a straight line. Rebuilding a network in a market you've been away from takes time, and I did it one mandate at a time.

      What I got in exchange is first-hand knowledge of how Cambodia, Thailand and Malaysia buy, how a Forex funnel or a Web3 community actually converts, and what travels back home. That is what I bring to the next team I work with.

      If that was the elephant, it's named now. Ask me anything about it.

      Ria
